SHATTERED REEF — BELLWETHER PHASE 2

Playable Island Redesign & Organic Art Implementation

You are acting as the lead environment artist, technical artist, world designer and gameplay engineer for Shattered Reef.

The objective is to turn the existing Bellwether Phase 1 geographical redesign into a fully integrated, playable island prototype, with substantially improved environmental artwork.

This is an implementation task, not another design-document exercise.

Work from branch:

design/bellwether-phase1

Preserve the work already completed. Do not regenerate the coastline from scratch unless a documented technical problem requires a targeted correction.

Read docs/ART_DIRECTION.md and inspect the approved production artwork before making visual decisions.

2. IMPLEMENT THE NEW ISLAND GEOGRAPHY

Replace Bellwether’s current coastline with the approved Phase 1 prototype geometry in an isolated game variant.

The island must have:

* Distinctive, naturally irregular shorelines.
* Headlands, coves, bays and inlets.
* Credible cliff formations.
* Natural beaches and shoreline transitions.
* Varied terrain elevation.
* Walkable hills and valleys.
* A functioning sheltered harbour.
* The existing freshwater lagoon.
* The existing separate islet.

Avoid simple circular, oval or polygonal islands.

The landmass must look deliberately authored rather than randomly generated.

Do not increase its area further. Review the existing 22% land-area increase and avoid unnecessary travel or empty terrain.

Ensure the terrain mesh, collision data, pathfinding grid and in-game maps all represent the same coastline.

3. ORGANIC, STYLISED ENVIRONMENT ARTWORK

This is one of the most important requirements.

Shattered Reef must not look like a game built from basic geometric primitives.

The target is stylised realism in shape, with clean and attractive artistic rendering.

We do not require photorealistic graphics.

We require convincing, organic shapes.

Trees

Trees must feature:

* Natural trunk forms.
* Believable branch structures.
* Irregular, asymmetrical canopies.
* Smooth, intentionally sculpted silhouettes.
* Species-appropriate proportions.
* Controlled variation between individual trees.

Do not use obvious cylinders topped with cones, spheres or stacked primitive shapes.

Rocks and cliffs

Rock formations must have:

* Organic erosion-inspired shapes.
* Smooth but irregular outlines.
* Convincing relationships between adjacent rock surfaces.
* Natural variation in scale and proportion.
* Restrained surface detailing.

Avoid visible primitive cubes, pyramids, faceted crystal-like rocks and random procedural blobs.

Buildings

Buildings must have:

* Convincing architectural proportions.
* Carefully shaped roofs and walls.
* Believable construction.
* Distinctive silhouettes.
* A consistent visual language appropriate to Bellwether’s settlement.

Avoid simplistic boxes with triangular roofs presented as finished buildings.

Coastlines and terrain

Coastlines must show credible transitions between land, beach, cliff, shallow water and deeper water.

Avoid abrupt artificial edges and obvious repeated procedural patterns.

Visual finish

Maintain:

* Soft, cohesive daylight.
* Matte materials.
* Restrained highlights.
* Attractive, consistent colours.
* Smooth-looking organic forms.
* Clear readability on mobile.
* The existing approved camera perspective and overall art direction.

Do not change Shattered Reef into a different visual genre.

4. ART PRODUCTION METHOD

Blender is not required.

Use the most suitable asset-production methods available in your environment, including:

* Authored or generated 3D models.
* GLB/glTF assets.
* Image generation for appropriate game assets.
* Carefully controlled textures.
* Custom organic meshes.
* Three.js rendering improvements.

Do not rely entirely on primitive geometry.

Do not present technically valid but visually crude models as finished artwork.

If the available tools cannot produce an asset at the required standard, report that limitation clearly and preserve the existing approved asset rather than replacing it with something inferior.

Artwork must be evaluated through actual rendered screenshots from the game, not only source images.

Do not bulk-replace the game’s approved artwork.

For this phase, concentrate on improving the visual quality of Bellwether.

7. MOBILE PERFORMANCE

Shattered Reef is mobile-first.

The redesigned island must remain practical on mobile devices.

Use appropriate asset complexity, geometry optimisation, instancing, texture sizes and draw-call management.

Evaluate:

* Rendering performance.
* Loading times.
* Memory usage.
* Collision and navigation performance.
* Touch-control responsiveness.

Do not sacrifice the required visual quality simply to achieve an arbitrary polygon count.

Use optimisation intelligently.

If real mobile hardware cannot be tested, state that limitation and report browser-based performance measurements separately.
