## Build loop (how every change is made)

1. Implement one feature in one batch of edits.
2. `npx tsc --noEmit && npx vitest run`: rules are pure TS, so whole play-throughs run headless in seconds.
3. `node scripts/e2e.cjs <dir>` against `npx vite`: boots in Chromium at 1280×800 and 390×844, fails on any console error. Touch/UI changes also run `node scripts/touch-e2e.cjs` (real held touches with state churn).
4. Screenshots only when visuals changed: `--shots` on e2e, or `node scripts/tour.cjs out.jpg` for a one-image contact sheet of every area (`SCENES=peak,fen` for a subset). Layout changes: `npx vite-node scripts/mapshot.ts out.ppm` draws the island top-down (walk grid, paths, nodes, spawns) in under a second, no browser.
5. Anything that adds things to the world: `node scripts/perf-budget.cjs` (phone screen, Max quality, 7 busiest places). Fails over 450 draw calls or 900k triangles per frame. Raise the budget only on purpose.
6. Commit when green. A stage is done only when its tests pass.

## Decisions
- **Art:** low-poly real-time 3D, fixed 3/4 camera (54°), look A "Bright" (see `docs/ART_DIRECTION.md`). All art built in code; no AI images.
- **Stack:** TypeScript + Vite + Three.js. Static hosting on GitHub Pages (`.github/workflows/deploy.yml` runs typecheck, tests, build, deploy on `main`).
- **Simulation is separate from rendering.** `src/game/**` has no Three.js import; the renderer reads state and listens to events. This is what makes headless tests (and later multiplayer) possible.
- **XP:** RuneScape curve continued to level 126; XP stored, level derived; 200M cap. Rates are RuneScape's: 4 XP per damage to the stance's skill, 1.33 to Hitpoints, early gathering values (copper 17.5, logs 25). Gathering on a 2.4 s rhythm.
- **Combat skills (RuneScape model):** Attack, Strength, Defence, Hitpoints, Ranged, Magic. Hit chance and max hit use the OSRS formulas; successful hits always do at least 1 (a 0 means a miss). Stances choose the trained skill. Combat triangle via per-style defence: crabs and the Guardian are weak to magic, brigands to ranged, gunners to melee. Combat level uses the OSRS formula (new character = 3).
- **Skills roadmap:** `docs/SKILLS.md` (12 in game, 22 planned). A skill ships only with real content.
- **Combat:** auto-attack anything in reach (Survivor.io feel), tap/click an enemy to chase it. Three active skills: dodge roll (3 s, no damage while rolling), whirl (6 s, hits everything within 3.2 m, knockback), flintlock (7 s, ranged). Enemy attacks are telegraphed; rolling through them is the skill play.
- ~~Starter island holds every skill's level range~~ (Tom, 2026-10-23; **reversed 2026-10-25**).
- **Islands in level order** (Tom, 2026-10-25, corrected the same day: "1-20 first island, 15-30 island 2, 25-50 island 3; more islands for 50+ that will be mixed difficulties"). Bands live on each region (`REGIONS[id].band`); a test holds every foe, boss (band top +2 at most) and gathering node inside them, and islands past 50 have no foe under 50.
  - Bellwether 1-20 (Smugglers' Caves 15-20, a stepping stone): copper, tin, iron; pine and oak; crabs, brigands, smugglers (retuned 24/28 to 18/20); the Guardian (22) as its finale.
  - Sunreach 15-30 (the Ember Deep 25-30): willow, oak, coal, iron; trout and salmon; mire crabs, boars, snakes; ember asps (retuned 54 to 28) and young weavers (new, 27) below; the Sunscale (retuned 60 to 32, bot-tested) as its finale.
  - Amberhold 25-50 (new, region `amberhold`, Tollhouse Landing outpost, Jory Penhallow's board and ferry): amber and willow; gold, stormsilver, coal; lobster and swordfish; boars, ironshell crabs, the poachers' camp, giant weavers in Weaver Hollow. No boss yet.
  - Stormwrack 50+ (mixed, moved east to x 1260): every 60-99 node; the drowned, their captain, stone sentinels. No boss yet.
  - Ferries list every route (price by distance). Sailing runs east-west along the chain.
  - Why Amberhold is new and Stormwrack kept (Tom picked "retune Stormwrack to 25-50, new 4th for 50+"): Stormwrack's wreck, crag, star crater and leviathan are all 50+ content; retuning it meant rebuilding all of that as island 4 anyway. Same bands, one island built instead of two.
- **Art standard (art pass 1, approved by Tom 2026-10-23):** smooth shading, blended vertex-colour terrain with speckle, cobbles and cloud shadows; item models rendered to slate-tile icons; loot as rarity-tinted sea-glass shards. Characters and buildings get the same treatment next.
- **No boxes in characters, UI or effects** (Tom, 2026-10-23: "boxy, like Minecraft"). Humanoids are turned profiles, capsules and spheres; UI panels are framed with brass filigree, tabs are medallions, orbs are round; particles are round.
- **Graphics pass 2 (2026-10-23):** image-based lighting from a painted sky (PMREM), baked terrain AO (horizon + contact shadows under every solid), world-space procedural surface detail (tonal grain + micro-bump) on props/buildings/characters, warm rim light on characters, bloom on emissives only. Battery saver turns detail and bloom off. Brightness measured against the previous build: +6–15%.
- **Characters pass 2 (2026-10-23):** one sculpted head per character wearing a face painted in code (eyes with irises and catch-lights, lids, brows, nose shading, lips, stubble, per-character eye colour); knee and elbow joints with a real walk cycle; collar, buttons, pouch, boot tops. Dialogue and the Equipment tab show 3D-rendered portraits. Zooming in lowers the camera up to 20 degrees so faces are visible in play.
- **Wear and repair (Tom asked, 2026-10-23):** weapons, head/body armour and tools wear (~1000 swings / ~1250 hits / ~2000 gathers). Worn out = 70% effect (tools act one tier lower); nothing breaks or vanishes. Repair at any anvil with one bar of the item's metal (+Smithing XP) or from Bram at the village smithy for ~30% of the lost value; affixes and rarity kept.
- **Workflow:** work accumulates on one branch; a PR is opened only when Tom says to put it live (a merged PR cannot be reused).
- **Characters: RuneScape-style low-poly** (Tom, 2026-10-24, from a reference image): realistic proportions, flat-shaded facets, ~1,800 triangles each (the vinyl toys were ~20,000), slightly oversized head and hands so faces read from above. `art/models/lowpoly.ts`; the vinyl toy style still exists (`style: 'toy'`) but nobody uses it.
- **No more graphics passes for now** (Tom, 2026-10-24: "maintain this level"). New content matches the current style.
- **Enterable buildings:** the simulation owns footprints, doors and furniture collision (`buildings.ts`, no Three.js); the renderer owns the cutaway. A building is "entered" when you're inside its walls (the doorway counts as outside, so the roof doesn't flicker).
- **One region at a time (Tom, 2026-10-24: "just one island that scales... a threshold crossed and a loading screen"):** replaced the seamless two-island world the same day. Each island and each cave is its own region with its own grids (`regions.ts`); only the one you're in is built and drawn. Sail off a charted edge (Bellwether east / Sunreach west), take the ferry, enter a cave mouth or climb its rope, and a loading screen covers the build. Builds: Bellwether 0.31 s, Sunreach 0.07 s, caves ~0.015 s (the old combined world was 0.85 s). Region layouts are cached, so going back is quicker. Saves are v4 and record the region; v3 saves from Sunreach are moved to its new position (x 190 -> 420).
- **Levels follow RuneScape (Tom, 2026-10-24: "follow the level up guide for RuneScape").** Unlock levels are RuneScape's wherever it has an equivalent: trees 1/15/30/45/60/75/90; ores copper/tin 1, iron 15, coal 30, gold 40; bars 1/15/30 (gold 40); the anvil gives a new item every few levels within a bar's tier (dagger +0, hatchet +1, pickaxe +2, helm +3, cutlass +5, pike +6, greatsword +14, plate +18); gear 1/1/5/10/20/30/40 then 50/60/70; axes and pickaxes 1/1/6/11/21/31/41/...; bows 1/5/20/30/40/50; fish and cooking levels, XP and burn-stop levels are RuneScape's; spells sit at RuneScape's levels for the same max hit (strikes 1-13 ... surges 81-95). Our own metals beyond rune (Cinderite, Skyglass, Leviathite, Orichalcum) fill 75-94. Perks kept on top (Tom's choice). The early-levels XP boost is gone (Tom chose RuneScape's pace); instead the starter tree, copper/tin and sprats are a little quicker and each level past a requirement adds 1.6% to success: level 15 in ~10-15 min with starter tools, 99 still 150+ h.
- **Health ×10 (Tom, 2026-10-24):** 100 health at the start, 990 at Hitpoints 99, like RuneScape 3. All damage, healing and enemy health scale with it (`HP_SCALE`); the data stays in RuneScape's small numbers and the game multiplies where it's used. A landed hit does 10..max, so average damage is exactly the old game's; XP is still 4 a point of unscaled damage. Saves are v5 (health ×10 on load).
- **Passive by default (Tom, 2026-10-24):** only camps and lairs (brigands, smugglers, poachers) and late-game zones (Wreckers' Point, the sentinels, the Ember Deep's weavers and asps) attack on sight (`EnemyDef.hostile`). Everything else fights back only when you attack it, and auto-attack no longer picks a fight with a passive creature you walk past. Hovering a hostile foe says "attacks on sight".
- **Twelve gear slots (Tom, 2026-10-25):** head, neck, ammo, ring, weapon, body, off-hand, ring, hands, legs, feet, wrist. The off-hand holds whatever suits the weapon's style: a shield (beside any one-handed weapon: blade, pistol or wand), a second blade (cutlass or dagger beside a cutlass or dagger), a second pistol, a second wand, or an orb or book (beside a wand). Two-handed weapons (staff, greatsword, pike, bows, crossbow) close it; a shield pushes a two-hander back to the pack. A second weapon strikes or fires with every attack at 50% of its own max hit, with its own accuracy, and the pair is 10% slower; pistols and wands use a second shot or a second spell's charms. Wand + orb equals a staff; a book trades damage for magic defence and a 10-25% chance a spell burns no charms. A full metal suit (helm, plate, legs, boots, gauntlets) adds up to what helm + plate gave before, so the shield is what a dual-wielder gives up; gauntlets add a little accuracy. The pistol brace is gone: v6 saves turn each brace into two pistols (worn: one in each hand; in the pack or strongbox: two). Armour still doesn't show on the character model (it never has); off-hand items do.
- **Keys and keyholes (Tom, 2026-10-25; first 10 built the same day):** keys found anywhere, extremely rarely (any gather, kill or new place; most often from the key's own line of work); a Key button (T) on the HUD; the Keyring in Equipment; a brass find card like a level-up; Settings > Developer: free key swaps. Save v7. `docs/KEYS.md`. Every human and creature has a keyhole. One key slot: slotted in you = a passive; turned in a foe within 3 m = an active power on a cooldown. Permanent keyring; swaps only at safe spots, never in a fight; a newly slotted key is dormant for 30 minutes of play (Tom), with a developer setting for free swaps. Quick Study (+3% skilling XP) is the starter. 21 keys designed, first batch of 10 to build after Crafting. Tom brought a 100-key list from ChatGPT; it was cut to these because most entries duplicated each other or existing gear bonuses, or needed systems the game doesn't have.
- **Bounties and crew (Tom, 2026-10-25; `data/bounties.ts`):** talking to a harbourmaster (Wynn, Sefa) opens their board: Bounties / Crew / Passage (the ferry moved here). Three contracts on each board, new ones every 30 minutes of play, carry up to 3: hunt 6-14 of a foe from that island or its caves (near your level), or one WANTED named elite (3× health, an elite affix, extra loot) that appears at a camp on the island while the contract is held. Contracts pay coins and bounty the moment they're done. Your bounty also rises for beating hostile foes (2 a level), hunters (5 a level) and bosses (Guardian 2,000, Sunscale 5,000); ranks Unknown 0, Troublemaker 300, Outlaw 1,500, Notorious 5,000, Dread captain 15,000, Terror of the Reef 40,000, Legend of the Shattered Sea 100,000 (wanted poster on the board and the Quests tab). From 5,000, bounty hunters ambush you every 15-25 minutes of play away from town (3 tiers, levels 22-70, by bounty). Crew: 6 sailors who want a minimum bounty and a fee (Nell the cook 0/150c, Jory the gunner 1,500/400, Sable the navigator 3,000/800, Big Oake the bosun 6,000/1,500, Wren the quartermaster 12,000/3,000, Old Kettle the rigger 25,000/6,000); every signed sailor's bonus counts (food, ranged damage, ship speed, health, prices, gathering); one first mate comes ashore, follows you and fights what you fight at 30-45% of your max hit (no XP for their hits). Boards and hunters roll on their own random stream. Save v8.
- **Signature weapons (Tom, 2026-10-25: "unique late weapons like the abyssal whip, our own"):** nine drop-only weapons, levels 70-85, each with one rule; always drop as relics, with a banner and fanfare. Undertow Lash (melee, 3 m reach, drags and slows; Drowned wreckers 1/400), Leviathan's Jaw (greatsword, every 4th swing bites twice and heals 5%; Drowned captain 1/250), Bellbreaker (pike, 25% stun 1.5 s; the Guardian 1/80), Sunscale Fang (dagger, venom: 1%/s for 4 s, no healing; the Sunscale 1/40), Locksmith's Blade (cutlass, +25% vs foes under a key power, kills take 5 s off the key's cooldown; wanted targets 1/25), Drowned Man's Flintlock (pistol, ricochet 50% to a second foe; famed bounty hunters 1/150), Stormcaller (crossbow, lightning to 2 more at 30%; stone sentinels 1/300), Skypiercer (longbow, arrows pierce the whole line at 70%; giant weavers 1/350), Maelstrom Staff (staff, a whirlpool drags foes within 2.5 m in for half the hit; ember asps 1/350). They use the existing weapon models recoloured (no whip model yet).
- **RuneScape-style drop tables (Tom, 2026-10-25: "think RuneScape's drop tables, some items from every creature, some unique"; `data/drops.ts`):** each creature keeps its own table (its unique drops: meat, hides, silk, snakeskin, sunscale, shell...), and every kill can also reach shared tables: the common table (1/4: coins, charms, a meal and ammo that suit the creature's level), the gem table (1/48: sea pearl 32, kelp jade 16, coral ruby 8, storm diamond 3, leviathan's eye 1), and from level 15 the rare drop table (1/96: big coin piles, gold bars, crystals, gems, caskets, geodes, charms). Elites reach them 3× as often; bosses always roll gems and the rare table. Mining turns up a rough gem 1 in 256 ores. Gems are polished at the bench (Crafting 20/27/34/43/55, RuneScape's XP) and set in gold at the furnace as rings, bracelets (+3) and amulets (+8).
- **Encounter upgrades (Survivor.io level-up choices):** not built yet; deliberately deferred so permanent skills stay the focus of the first island.

## Controls
- **Phone:** drag anywhere to move or steer; tap things to walk to them and use them; big round button = nearest action; small buttons = roll, whirl, flintlock; pinch = zoom; tap minimap or map to walk there.
- **Laptop:** click to walk/use (RuneScape-style); right-click items for details; WASD/arrows move; E action; Space dodge roll; R whirl; F flintlock; I G K J M O tabs; Esc closes; scroll zooms.
